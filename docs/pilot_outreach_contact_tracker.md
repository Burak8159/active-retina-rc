## Outreach Timing

Only send after all of the following are ready:

- Professor-reviewed or professor-endorsed manuscript package.
- Updated arXiv/IEEE manuscript drafts.
- Product demo screenshots or short video.
- Validation summary with exact claim boundary.
- Pilot protocol outline.
- Data governance and safety statement.

## Target Ask

Initial ask should be low-risk:

1. Retrospective validation on de-identified retinal images under the institution's governance.
2. Clinician-reviewed workflow evaluation.
3. No autonomous patient-care decision-making in the first pilot.
4. Optional prospective study only after retrospective evidence and institutional approval.

## Avoid

- Do not say "ready for clinical rollout."
- Do not claim "fully autonomous diagnosis" unless the final untouched evidence supports it.
- Do not offer to share restricted PhysioNet/mBRSET data.
- Do not imply the model can make patient-care decisions during the pilot.
