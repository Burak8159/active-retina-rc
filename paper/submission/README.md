## Current Evidence

The separate IEEE review snapshot remains an internal review archive and is not the target deliverable in this preprint pass. The current aggregate-only snapshot is v116: [`active_retina_ieee_review_bundle_2026-10-19_v116.zip`](active_retina_ieee_review_bundle_2026-10-19_v116.zip), with checksum sidecar [`active_retina_ieee_review_bundle_2026-10-19_v116.zip.sha256`](active_retina_ieee_review_bundle_2026-10-19_v116.zip.sha256) and verification receipt [`verification_2026-10-19_v116.json`](verification_2026-10-19_v116.json).

- The primary raw-image result is the frozen ResNet-50 cached-feature classifier on MMRDR's official within-dataset UWF held-out partition: 1,283/1,288 referable images detected, 5 false-negative images, 262/1,309 nonreferable images cleared, 20.02% specificity (image-level Wilson 95% CI 17.94--22.27%), and 89.72% referred. The released project rows do not contain patient IDs, so patient-level separation cannot be independently verified here. This is not external-site, prospective, or clinical validation.
- A post-hoc SHA-256 scan of 10,404 local UWF images found eight exact-duplicate groups; two crossed official train/test, and both test copies were positive and referred with exact model-fit copies. Excluding these rows descriptively leaves five misses and 20.02% specificity unchanged. This does not restore an independent test, and exact-byte matching cannot establish participant identity or detect transformed near-duplicates.
- The 70.13% figure is a different result: deterministic replay of a frozen score policy on an exposed 981-eye cohort, with 270/385 gradable nonreferable eyes cleared and no observed referable/ungradable clearances. It is not raw-image model performance or an independent estimate.
- A paired analysis of the same exposed cohort gives 69.61% (268/385) after patient-grouped calibration, a -0.52-point eye-level specificity difference from replay (conditional patient-cluster interval -1.31 to 0.00). Among 596 referable or ungradable eye records, missed required eyes are 0/596 under replay and 3/596 under grouped calibration; the paired eye-level missed-required-eye-rate difference is +0.50 points (conditional interval 0.00--1.17). The interval includes zero, is conditional on fixed decisions, and is not confirmatory.
- New source-composition analysis reports source-stratified patient-cluster intervals: DDR 65.05% (95% interval 58.25--71.36), DeepDRiD 98.08% (93.75--100.00; 31 negative-patient clusters), and DRTiD 66.93% (58.27--74.80). Exploratory DeepDRiD contrasts are positive, while the DDR--DRTiD interval includes zero. The equal-source shift from 70.13% to 76.68% decomposes to -13.12, +19.45, and +0.23 percentage points by DDR, DeepDRiD, and DRTiD reweighting, respectively. These are conditional descriptions of the selected exposed sample, not causal source effects or improved performance.
- The 84.16% multi-source accuracy is a post-selection threshold-crossfit diagnostic with 113 false negatives; the 47.15% ConvNeXt-Tiny result is exposed development evidence. Neither is confirmatory validation or directly comparable to the MMRDR held-out result.
- Core references on selective classification, selective prediction, model-selection bias, and DR domain generalization now include publisher/proceedings landing-page links; volume and issue details were checked against NeurIPS, PMLR, JMLR, and CVF records.
